Local Dent Removal You Can Trust In Chippenham

Chippenham is one of our busiest towns outside Melksham, and it is not hard to see why. It is a big town with a lot of parked cars in a small centre, and almost all of the damage we get called to starts in a bay that is narrower than the car standing in it.

The multi storey at Borough Parade and the parking around Emery Gate account for a steady stream of door dings. Concrete pillars are the other one there, and a pillar rarely leaves a neat round dent. It leaves a crease down a door or a rear wing that needs easing out in stages rather than pushing out in one go.

Then there is the station. Cars parked up at seven in the morning and left until the evening train get bumped by every other commuter squeezing into the space alongside. Out in the newer estates at Cepen Park, Pewsham and Monkton Park the driveways are shorter and the entrances tighter, which is where the wall scrapes and folded door edges come from.

No paint. No filler. No respray. Your car keeps its original factory finish, and its history stays clean.

The Process

How It Works

Four steps from the photo on your phone to the dent being gone. No garage visit just to find out what it costs.

  • Step One Send Us A Photo Take a clear picture or a short video of the damage. Get the whole panel in frame and catch the light across the dent, because that is what shows us the depth.
  • Step Two We Come Back With A Price We look at what you have sent, tell you straight whether PDR will put it right, and give you a price. No trip to a garage just to get quoted.
  • Step Three Book A Time That Suits You Happy with the price? We put you in the diary at a time that works around your week rather than ours.
  • Step Four We Come To You We come to you in Chippenham at a time that suits, carry out the repair, and leave the original paint exactly as it was.
