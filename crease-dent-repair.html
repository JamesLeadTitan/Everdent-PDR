The Repair

What To Expect

A crease is reversed in the opposite order to how it happened. This is what that looks like.

  • We find both endsThe ends of a crease are where the metal is least distorted, and that is where the work starts. Going straight for the deepest point is the quickest way to leave a permanent high spot.
  • Pressure is released along the foldThe crease is worked in overlapping passes from each end towards the middle, releasing the tension a little at a time. The line lifts gradually rather than popping out in one go.
  • High spots are knocked back as we goMetal displaced by the fold sits proud on either side. It is tapped down repeatedly during the repair, not left until the end, so the panel stays level as the crease comes up.
  • The panel is read across, not alongFinished creases are judged by running the reflection across the line at ninety degrees. If the light passes over without breaking, the fold is genuinely gone.

Creases take longer than their size suggests, and a stubborn one can be most of a day. Worked properly the panel keeps its original paint and there is nothing to cure before you drive it.
