The Repair

What To Expect

Door edges are fiddly, close in work. This is the order it happens in.

  • The fold is assessed from both sidesWe look at the outer skin and the inner return, because an edge that has folded has moved in two directions at once. Only working the outside would leave the lip sitting proud.
  • The lip is opened graduallyFine tools ease the fold back out a little at a time, working along the damage rather than at the worst point. Rushing the middle of a fold is what cracks paint that had survived the impact.
  • The edge is trued along its lengthOnce the fold is out, the whole lip is checked for straightness with the door open and shut. A repaired edge that catches the seal or sits proud of the wing is not finished.
  • The door is checked shutIt closes cleanly, sits flush, and the gap either side matches. We look at all of that before you do.

Most door edge repairs are a one to three hour job depending on how hard the impact was. The car goes straight back into normal use afterwards with nothing to cure or avoid.
