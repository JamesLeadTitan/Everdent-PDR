What It Costs

What You'll Pay - No Hidden Costs

Every door ding is a slightly different job, so there is no one size fits all price on this page. What there is instead is a straight price from a photo, before anybody books anything in.

Three things decide what a ding costs to put right: how wide it is, how deep the metal has been pushed, and where on the panel it landed. A shallow ding in the open middle of a door is a quick job. The same ding sitting on a swage line, or right behind a brace where access is tight, takes longer and is priced for the extra work.

You get a fixed price before we start, and that is the price you pay. If we look at the ding and PDR will not put it right on its own, we tell you before any work begins rather than halfway through.

The Repair

What To Expect

Here is what actually happens to a door ding, in plain English rather than trade talk.

  • We read the dent before we touch itA reflection board goes up against the panel and the light lines across it show the exact shape and depth of the ding. That reading is what decides where the first push goes.
  • We get behind the panelAccess usually means dropping the door card or coming in through a factory access hole. Nothing is drilled and nothing is cut. If a panel genuinely cannot be reached, we say so rather than forcing it.
  • The metal comes back graduallySpecialist rods work the ding out in small, controlled pushes rather than one big shove. Steel has a memory, and worked slowly it goes back where it started with the paint intact.
  • You look at it before we call it finishedThe panel gets checked under the board again, then you look over it yourself in daylight. We are not done until you are happy with what you are looking at.

Most single door dings are finished inside a couple of hours, and the car is ready to drive straight away. There is no curing, no drying and no coming back next week to collect it.
