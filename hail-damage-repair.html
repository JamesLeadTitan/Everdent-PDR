What It Costs

What You'll Pay - No Hidden Costs

Hail is priced per car rather than per dent, and it is the one job where we would always rather see the vehicle in person before committing to a figure.

The count matters, but so does where the dents sit. Dents in the open middle of a roof are quick. Dents along the edge of a bonnet, up against a brace, or on a double skinned panel all take longer because access is restricted. Aluminium panels, which a lot of modern bonnets are, work harder than steel and add time again.

You get one fixed price for the car before we start, not a running total that climbs as the day goes on. If some panels are beyond PDR, they come off the quote and we tell you which ones and why.

The Repair

What To Expect

Hail is the longest job we do, and it is methodical rather than dramatic.

  • The car is counted panel by panelUnder proper lighting, each panel is mapped and the dents marked. That count is what tells us how long the car will take and which panels need trim or headlining moved.
  • Access is opened upHeadlining may need dropping for a roof, and bonnet liners or boot trim removed. It is done properly and put back properly, and nothing is drilled.
  • Each dent is pushed out individuallyThere is no shortcut here. Every dent gets its own sequence of small pushes, working across the panel in a set order so nothing is missed and the panel stays flat overall.
  • The panel is checked as a wholeOnce a panel is done it goes back under the board and gets read across its full width. Hail repair is judged on how the light runs over the whole roof, not on any single dent.

A lightly hit car can be a day. A badly hit one runs to several. We will give you a realistic timescale up front, along with which panels are being treated, so you can plan around being without the car.
