What It Costs

What You'll Pay - No Hidden Costs

This is the work that gets quoted most carefully, because from a photograph a severe dent can look better or worse than it really is. Send photos and expect us to ask a couple of questions back.

Price follows the state of the metal. A large soft dent in the middle of a panel can be less work than a small cluster sitting across a bodyline. What pushes a job up is sharpness, how many dents overlap, how far the metal has been stretched, and whether the panel is aluminium, which moves very differently to steel.

You get one fixed price before anything starts, and an honest view on what the panel will look like at the end. On damage this size we would rather turn work away than hand back a repair we are not happy to put our name to.

The Repair

What To Expect

Severe damage is a staged repair. Nothing about it is fast, and that is deliberate.

  • The damage is mapped before anything movesUnder the reflection board we work out where the metal is stretched, where it is simply displaced, and which part of the damage is holding the rest in place. That last point decides where we start.
  • The panel is worked in passesThe deepest area comes up a fraction at a time across several passes, with high spots tapped down between each one. Trying to take a severe dent out in one push is what stretches metal and ruins panels.
  • Sharp detail is left until lastCreases, bodylines and the sharpest folds are addressed once the surrounding metal is back roughly where it belongs, because they will not hold their shape until it is.
  • The finish is judged in daylightPanel read across and along under the board, then checked outside. On severe work we will point out anything we can still see rather than waiting for you to find it.

Expect severe and complex work to take the best part of a day, sometimes more. You get a realistic timescale up front, and an honest picture of the final result before you decide to go ahead.
